## Original Zork

**Zork** was created by Marc Blank, Dave Lebling, Bruce Daniels, and Tim Anderson. This edition draws on Infocom's Zork I source, which Microsoft released under MIT in November 2025 through the existing [historicalsource/zork1 repository](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1). See [Microsoft's release announcement](https://opensource.microsoft.com/blog/2025/11/20/preserving-code-that-shaped-generations-zork-i-ii-and-iii-go-open-source/).

Room descriptions, object descriptions, dialogue, and selected responses come from revision [`97b7b3d68c075dd9af7da499c3e9690ada3471fd`](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1/tree/97b7b3d68c075dd9af7da499c3e9690ada3471fd), particularly `1dungeon.zil`, `1actions.zil`, `gverbs.zil`, and `gglobals.zil`. Directions and changing-state clauses are adapted to the condensed 3D geography. Additional prose supports the modern interactions.
